Beginner Class Guide

Not sure where to start? Here's a quick breakdown of the seven classes.

**Melee**

- Sword Warrior — high burst, close range
- Blade Specialist — fast, mobile duelist

**Ranged**

- Archer — sustained damage from afar
- Mage — area control and crowd control

**Support**

- Priest — healing and buffs
- Bard — utility and team scaling

__Pick what fits your playstyle, not just the meta.__

Experiment in the training grounds before committing your build.
