Safe Trading with a Middleman

Always use a __verified middleman__ for big trades. It only takes a minute and prevents scams.

**The process**

- Both players agree on a verified middleman
- The seller hands the item to the middleman
- The buyer sends payment to the middleman
- The middleman releases item and payment at the same time

~~Never trade directly with someone you don't trust.~~ Even friends can be compromised — use a middleman for high-value deals.
